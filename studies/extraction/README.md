# Study I: expert review of extracted rules (Sect. 4)

`expert_review.csv`: the 18 review rows with a verdict (pledge 9, lien 5, possession 1, general contract provisions 3),
covering 102 rules. Columns: workbook sheet, row, section, rule keys, verdict (确认 confirmed / 小改后确认 confirmed after
minor revision / 待确认 pending), the reviewer's reasons and supplementary sources. Reviewer identity is withheld.
The rules themselves are in `resources/civil/civil_review.lrml`.
